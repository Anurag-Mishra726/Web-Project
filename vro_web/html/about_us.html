About Us

The Eldvar Institute (EI) & Frontier Research Hub (FRH) is a multidisciplinary research institution established in 2019. This institute is dedicated to historical research, geopolitics, defense, and economic analysis. EI & FRH seeks to provide accurate, evidence-based research while challenging distorted narratives and misinformation.

  • Eldvar Institute (EI) - Focused on historical reasearch, myth debunking, and cultural analysis.
  • Forntier Reaserarch Hub (FRH) - Specializing in geopolitics, defense strategies, and economic policies, particularly concenring India's strategic interests.

Mission & Objectives

  1. Historical Research & Myth Debunking (Through Eldvar Institute)
    • Investigating misrepresented historical narratives and reconstructing factual accounts.
    • Studying ancient civilizations, warfare, governance, and cultural evolution.
    • Correcting colonial distortions and political biases in history.
  2. Geopolitical & Strategic Studies (Through Frontier Research Hub)
    • Analyzing global power shifts and international relations.
    • Researching India's border security, defense modernization, and maritime strategy.
    • Examining China and Pakistan's influence on India's security and economy.
  3. Economic & Policy Research
    • Studying India's defense economy, strategic investments, and trade policies.
    • Assessing economic warfare tactics and their geopolitical implications.
    • Providing insights into global financial trends affecting India's future.

Eldvar Institute (EI)

The Eldvar Institute (EI) is a specialized research institution focused on historical research, myth debunking,and the study of civilizations, warfare, and cultural evolution. It is committed to reconstructing historicalnarratives based on evidence and challenging distorted accounts. The name "Eldvar" symbolizes the pursuit oftruth, wisdom, and enlightenment. While it does not have a direct meaning, it can be interpreted as acombination of:

  • "Eld" (Old Norse for "fire" or "wisdom")
  • "Var" (Latin/Norse for "truth" or "protection")

This represents the institute's commitment to uncovering historical truths and preserving knowledge.

Forntier Research Hub (FRH)

The Frontier Research Hub (FRH) is a premier research institute specializing in geopolitics, defense, andeconomic analysis. FRH is dedicated to providing strategic insights, policy recommendations, and data-drivenresearch on national and international affairs. We operate at the intersection of global strategy and analyticalresearch, producing high-quality reports that guide decision-makers, policymakers, and researchers inunderstanding the complexities of global power dynamics.
